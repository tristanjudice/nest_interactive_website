INTERGALACTIC PUZZLE TOUCHDOWN (Q3 2026)

Since our earliest recorded history, mankind has dreamed about life amongst the stars. Well, it turns out aliens are real and that they've intercepted all of our broadcasts of The Big Game. Now they're reshaping their society around "foobaw" and duking it to see who comes out on top.

Intergalactic Puzzle Touchdown is an asymmetric turn-based PvP puzzle game that combines familiar mechanics of classic match-3 style games with the pulse-pounding excitement of gridiron football.

  • Match tiles advance towards the End Zone
  • Think ahead to block your opponent's best moves
  • Push your luck with powerful Big Plays and risky Penalties
  • Use unique Team Powers to warp the game rules

Start out by tackling series of interactive tutorials to learn the ropes, then pick your favorite team and hone your skills in Quick Match against a variety of challenging CPU opponents. Once you're ready, you can also hop online to face-off against the best-of-the-best players from around the globe.
